📍 London

Navigating London’s City, Canary Wharf, and tech hubs tests cognitive stamina and emotional endurance daily. In high-velocity environments like Google, Meta, or venture-backed fintechs, professionals operate under relentless performance expectations.

Yet sustained excellence often masks silent exhaustion: standard weekends fail to recharge your energy, communicating in a second language brings subtle impostor friction, and the boundary between work and rest quietly dissolves.

Key challenges we address:

  • Executive & Tech Burnout: Prolonged fatigue where standard weekends no longer restore energy, accompanied by cynicism and cognitive depletion.
  • Imposter Syndrome & Linguistic Friction: The subtle internal strain of leading and negotiating in a second language, feeling less nuanced or persuasive than native counterparts.
  • High-Performance Overdrive: Inability to switch off from work, constant sympathetic nervous system activation, and compromised sleep quality.
  • Expat Dislocation & Lack of Grounding: Navigating London’s fast-paced environment without an established emotional support system.
  • Sponsorship & Uncertainty Stress: Performance anxiety exacerbated by Skilled Worker Visa dependency and economic restructuring.

The Three-Pillar Methodology: Brain — Mind — Body

My methodology bridges rigorous academic scholarship (Dr. phil., Europa-Universität Flensburg, magna cum laude), accredited practical psychology training (1,080 hours, MPGU), certified neurointegration (250 hours), and physiological stress regulation via the Oxygen Advantage® system (Certified Functional Breathing Instructor Level 1).

We intervene simultaneously across three levels:

  1. Cognitive Domain: Practical CBT and ACT protocols to defuse catastrophic thinking and dismantle perfectionistic self-sabotage.
  2. Neurobiological Domain: Aligning daily workflows with circadian and dopamine rhythms to restore prefrontal cortex function.
  3. Somatic Domain: Targeted autonomic regulation via scientifically grounded functional breathwork and gentle somatic release to restore parasympathetic ease and deep sleep.

I deliver individual sessions online via secure Google Meet (60 minutes). Session fee: £90 (~€105). In-person consulting sessions in central London are available during scheduled visiting periods by prior individual arrangement.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the regulatory status of the practice in the UK? +

The service is provided as Psychological Counselling, Neurointegration & Executive Resilience Coaching. In the UK, HCPC statutory regulation strictly covers clinical psychologists. Our non-clinical practice is backed by an accredited Dr. phil. (Germany) and comprehensive postgraduate psychology training.

How are sessions held and what tools are used? +

Consultations are 60 minutes long and take place via secure Google Meet or Zoom. We focus on pragmatic CBT protocols, nervous system regulation, and functional breathwork.

What currencies and payment methods are accepted? +

Payments are accepted in GBP (£) or EUR (€) via bank transfer, Revolut, Stripe, or PayPal with formal receipts provided.

Are in-person sessions in London possible? +

Yes, occasional in-person sessions in central London consulting rooms are available during Nataliia's visiting periods by prior individual agreement.
