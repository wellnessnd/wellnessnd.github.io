Questions and answers

Short answers before an initial enquiry.

Submit an enquiry

If your question is not listed, submit a short enquiry through the form or message me on Telegram @natali_tvet_pro. I will get back to you personally within one to two working days.

How does neurointegration differ from traditional psychotherapy?

Neurointegration is rooted in applied neuroscience and nervous system states. Rather than focusing on prolonged analysis of past history, this approach targets practical, real-time self-regulation: calming the physiological stress response through body and breathwork, restoring cognitive bandwidth, and establishing sustainable daily habits.

How does the first consultation work, and can I start with just one session?

Yes, you can start with a single consultation. The 60-minute session takes place via video call (Google Meet/Zoom) or in person in Belgrade. We explore your current circumstances, diagnose stress levels and cognitive depletion, and establish a personalized recovery plan. Continuing is always mutually agreed upon; committing to a multi-week package is not mandatory.

How many sessions are typically needed to see noticeable results?

A single consultation provides immediate relief from acute overwhelm and provides a clear personal plan. For sustainable habit change and overcoming burnout, the four-week neurointegration program (4 sessions plus messaging support) or a 5-session package is recommended.

Is there support between sessions?

Messaging support (guidance, answering questions, habit tracking via Telegram) is included in the four-week neurointegration program. For single consultations, continuous messaging between meetings is not included so you can independently integrate the recommendations.

Which conditions and issues do you not work with?

I do not work with clinical diagnoses (clinical depression, bipolar disorder, PTSD), substance dependencies, eating disorders, or with children and couples. These fall under clinical medicine and psychiatry. In acute health or crisis emergencies, please contact emergency services immediately (112).

What formats are consultations available in (online worldwide & in-person in Belgrade)?

Consultations are conducted online for adult clients (18+) worldwide (frequently working with expats in Belgrade, London, Berlin, Barcelona, Limassol, Tbilisi, Yerevan, and Moscow). Regular in-person sessions are held in Belgrade by advance booking.

How do I distinguish emotional burnout from everyday tiredness?

Normal fatigue disappears after restorative sleep or a restful weekend. In burnout, standard rest no longer recharges you: emotional depletion persists, accompanied by detachment, background anxiety, and cognitive fog. If this continues for weeks, the nervous system requires systematic self-regulation and resource rebuilding.

How do brain, psyche, and body connect in practice (the triad approach)?

We do not merely discuss challenges cognitively. When physical tension is chronic and breathing is shallow, the nervous system remains trapped in fight-or-flight mode. We therefore integrate gentle somatic exercises and targeted breathwork to regulate the physiological stress response, while psychological methods establish cognitive clarity and lasting habits without self-coercion.
