Behind Barcelona’s coastal lifestyle and the tech hub of the 22@ district lies a demanding reality for software engineers, product leads, and international founders.

Many professionals experience what is known as “Sunny Burnout”: an acute internal contrast between bright Mediterranean surroundings and relentless screen fatigue. When you work 12-hour days behind closed shutters while feeling guilty for not enjoying the city, exhaustion quickly compounds.

Common challenges for tech specialists and expats in Barcelona:

  • “Sunny Burnout” in High-Growth Tech: Cognitive exhaustion, diminished creativity, and guilt over feeling unhappy while living in a Mediterranean destination.
  • Nomad Visa & Regulatory Friction: Administrative bottlenecks (Cita previa), tax ambiguities (Beckham Law vs Autónomo), and background residency renewal anxieties.
  • Expat Isolation Behind the Screen: The honeymoon phase gives way to feeling isolated within an international bubble, missing long-standing personal networks.
  • Circadian Misalignment & Physical Tension: Racing thoughts before sleep, jaw clenching, shallow breathing, and difficulty disconnecting from asynchronous team chats.
  • Relocation Career Recalibration: Questioning work meaning, identity shifts, and redefining personal success in a new cultural environment.

The Triad Framework: Brain — Mind — Body

My integrative practice unites rigorous academic foundations (Dr. phil., Germany; state postgraduate psychology diploma MPGU, 1080 hours), certified Neurointegration (250 hours), and somatic self-regulation:

  1. Cognitive Rebalancing (CBT): Disentangling performance guilt, cultivating realistic self-expectations, and restoring cognitive clarity.
  2. Neurointegration Protocols: Calibrating dopamine and energy expenditure rhythms, restoring prefrontal executive bandwidth.
  3. Oxygen Advantage® & Somatic Movement: Regulating autonomic tone through functional breathwork, releasing chronic diaphragmatic tension, and deepening sleep quality.
