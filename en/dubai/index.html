Scaling a venture or leading international operations in Dubai demands relentless stamina. Global time zones, high-stakes deal-making, and round-the-clock networking keep the nervous system in a continuous state of sympathetic hyper-arousal.

Yet rapid commercial growth frequently masks internal fatigue: outward convenience and luxury paired with chronic sensory overload, fragmented sleep, and an absence of genuine restorative space.

Key challenges for founders and professionals in Dubai:

  • Executive & High-Performer Burnout: Running on adrenaline and discipline while feeling completely drained of authentic motivation and creative energy.
  • 24/7 Global Deal-Making & Jet Lag Stress: Blurring work-life boundaries across international time zones, compromised sleep architecture, and inability to switch off mentally.
  • Imposter Syndrome on the Global Stage: Intense psychological pressure when negotiating alongside prominent international investors, founders, and executives.
  • Transience & The Social Connection Deficit: Navigating transactional relationships and expat turnover without deep, grounded emotional safety.
  • Physical Dysregulation: Tension headaches, shallow breathing, caffeine dependence, and fatigue from prolonged indoor and flight environments.

The Integrative Architecture: Brain — Mind — Body

My practice combines rigorous academic research (Dr. phil., Germany), comprehensive postgraduate psychology training (MPGU, 1080 hours), certified Neurointegration (250 hours), and certified physiological breathwork through Oxygen Advantage® (Level 1 Certified Instructor):

  1. Cognitive Domain: Reframing hyper-responsibility, unravelling cognitive distortions, and establishing sustainable boundaries.
  2. Neurobiological Domain: Regulating prefrontal cortex function, mastering rapid transitions between focused execution and deep recovery.
  3. Somatic Domain: Down-regulating the autonomic nervous system through scientifically grounded functional breathing protocols and tension release.

I hold individual sessions online via secure Google Meet (60 minutes), accommodating UAE time (Gulf Standard Time / GST, UTC+4). Consultation fee: €100 ($110) with official digital invoices provided.
