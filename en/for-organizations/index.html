Team Resilience & Stress-Management Programs

Applied corporate workshops, intensives, and burnout prevention programs grounded in neurobiology and respiratory physiology.

Request an organisational proposal

I design science-backed corporate programs in applied self-regulation, cognitive load management, and burnout prevention for modern teams and leaders.

My corporate programmes are grounded in applied physiology and neurobiology: respiratory biomechanics (certified Oxygen Advantage® protocols), autonomic nervous system regulation, and cognitive focus cycles. Teams master practical, desk-compatible tools (taking 60 seconds to 3 minutes) that work directly during demanding delivery sprints, back-to-back meetings, or high-stakes presentations.

Professional Credentials

  • Academic Rigour: Doctor of Philosophy in Adult Education & TVET (Dr. phil., Europa-Universität Flensburg, Germany) and Candidate of Economic Sciences (PhD equivalent, Innovation Management);
  • Evidence-Based Practice: Certified Practical Psychologist (MPGU diploma, 1,080 hours) and Certified Neurointegration Trainer (250 hours);
  • International Physiological Certification: Certified Functional Breathing Instructor Oxygen Advantage® (Level 1, ACE Provider); certified in Oxygen Advantage® for Corporates (OA® for Corporates) (Patrick McKeown, November 2026);
  • Extensive Background: 15+ years designing adult educational frameworks, 40+ professional development programs developed, 2,000+ leaders and specialists trained, supporting digital transformation teams.

Delivery Formats

  1. Introductory Interactive Workshop (60–90 minutes) The recommended pilot format to introduce teams to practical self-regulation tools. We examine stress physiology, tunnel vision, and cognitive fatigue, practicing 3–4 actionable micro-drills (heart rate down-regulation, 90-second cognitive reset). Pilot conditions: €350–€500 for up to 20–25 participants. Delivered on-site at your Belgrade office or online for distributed teams.

  2. Team Resilience Intensive (3.5–4 hours) In-depth examination of team stress factors, communication, and cognitive recovery. Mapping workflow bottlenecks causing cognitive friction, deep physiological recovery protocols, somatic drills for relieving computer-related postural tension, and co-creating a sustainable “team resilience contract.”

  3. 4-Week Cohort Resilience Track Systematic habit integration into daily company workflows. Four weekly interactive 75-minute sessions, asynchronous micro-habit tracking in a dedicated internal channel, a library of audio guides, and an aggregate outcome assessment for the People/HR team.

  4. Executive Wellbeing Advisory (1-on-1 for Leaders) Confidential, high-touch advisory for founders, C-level executives, and engineering leads. One-on-one sessions (online or in Belgrade) focused on preserving cognitive stamina, preventing leadership exhaustion, and optimizing personal recovery rhythms.

How We Work Together

  1. Initial Brief: Complete the form below, outlining your team size, working format and core goals.
  2. 20-Minute Alignment Call: We discuss your team’s schedule, preferred language (English or Russian), and format (on-site in Belgrade or remote).
  3. Delivery & Actionable Insights: Following the workshop or programme, I aggregate anonymous participant feedback and deliver practical recommendations for leadership and People teams.
