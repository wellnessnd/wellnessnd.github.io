Client Informed Consent

Professional boundaries, principles of collaboration, safety, and mutual obligations.

Current Edition: July 2026 ↓ Download document (.docx)

By accepting this Informed Consent, I confirm the following:

  1. Age and Capacity: I am at least 18 years of age, legally competent, and make the decision to participate voluntarily.
  2. Non-Medical Nature of Services: I understand that I am receiving non-medical psychological and informational consulting services.
  3. No Medical Diagnosis or Prescription: I understand that the practitioner does not provide medical or psychiatric care, does not make clinical diagnoses, does not prescribe medication, and does not alter doctor's prescriptions.
  4. Not an Emergency Service: I understand that psychological consultations are not crisis or emergency intervention. In situations involving immediate risk to life or health, I undertake to contact emergency services (e.g. 112) or medical institutions immediately.
  5. Potential Emotional Reactions: I understand that the process may involve strong emotions, temporary discomfort, fatigue, resistance, and re-evaluation of relationships and life choices.
  6. Right to Pause and Refuse: I have the right to ask questions, report discomfort, refuse specific exercises, take a pause, or terminate the consultation at any time.
  7. No Guarantee of Specific Outcomes: Specific outcomes cannot be guaranteed. Results depend on the nature of the request, client engagement, regularity, personal characteristics, and circumstances beyond the practitioner's control.
  8. Personal Autonomy and Responsibility: I make decisions independently and take responsibility for actions taken outside of consultations.
  9. Disclosure of Health Conditions: I undertake to inform the practitioner of significant medical conditions, pregnancy, medications, or any circumstances that may affect the safety of psychological, neurointegration, body-oriented, or breathwork practices.
  10. Confidentiality and Exceptions: I understand confidentiality standards and lawful exceptions (threat to life, statutory disclosure obligations).
  11. Recording Policy: Individual sessions are never recorded without explicit prior consent from both parties.
  12. Acceptance of Service Terms: I have reviewed the Service Agreement (Public Offer), had the opportunity to ask questions, and agree to its terms.
