Book a consultation

A short enquiry, format confirmation, secure payment and personal scheduling.

Booking options & contacts
  1. Select a convenient slot via our secure booking service DIKIDI (with instant SMS confirmation) or outline your enquiry in the short form below.
  2. I personally review your request to ensure my approach and format suit your needs.
  3. You can pay securely online by card (supporting both international and Russian bank cards via Lava.top / Tribute) or via direct bank transfer.
  4. Once payment is confirmed, your appointment is secured and I will email your Google Meet video link.

Each consultation lasts 60 minutes (€100 online / €120 in person). The practice is dedicated to adult clients aged 18 and older. Online consultations are available worldwide (regularly working with clients in London, Barcelona, Berlin, Limassol, Tbilisi, Yerevan, Moscow and Belgrade); regular in-person appointments take place in Belgrade, with occasional sessions in London during visits by prior arrangement.

If you cancel with more than 24 hours’ notice, you can choose between a full refund or rescheduling to another date. For late cancellations (under 24 hours) or no-shows, fees are non-refundable; unforeseen emergencies are handled individually.
