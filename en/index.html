Brain — Psyche — Body Online worldwide / In person in Belgrade

I help adults understand their inner state, recover resources when faced with overload and burnout, and master reliable self-regulation skills.

Working in adult education and research for over 10 years, I observed a consistent pattern: we can spend a lot of time analyzing stress at a cognitive level, but if the body is tense, breathing is shallow, and the nervous system is depleted, cognitive tools often falter. That is why my practice unites three levels: Brain — Psyche — Body.

My methodology is rooted in an evidence-based approach, combining practical psychology, neurointegration, breathwork, and gentle qigong movement. As a researcher and educator, I translate complex scientific findings into clear, manageable actions without pressure or burnout.

Credentials and verified background: Dr. phil. (Germany, magna cum laude), Candidate of Economic Sciences (Russia), Practical psychologist (MPGU diploma, 1080h), Certified Neurointegration Trainer (250h), Certified Oxygen Advantage® Functional Breathing Instructor (Level 1 & Corporate), Health Qigong Instructor (Ba Duan Jin), 15+ years in adult education and research.

Common reasons to reach out:

  • Emotional and professional burnout: depleted energy, chronic fatigue, and loss of motivation when standard rest no longer restores you.
  • Chronic stress and anxiety: inner tension, sleep disruption, difficulty winding down, and racing thoughts.
  • Impostor syndrome and self-doubt: discounting achievements, fear of not meeting expectations in new roles, and harsh self-criticism.
  • Difficulty focusing and procrastination: scattered attention, delaying important actions, and lack of clarity.
  • Adjustment after relocation and life transitions: migration stress, adapting to new environments, or redefining personal and professional anchors.
  • Developing self-regulation skills: establishing sustainable habits and moving toward your goals at a steady, supportive pace.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers regarding methodology, session formats, preparation, and practice boundaries.

How does neurointegration differ from traditional psychotherapy?

In my practice, I combine applied neuroscience with somatic self-regulation. While psychotherapy often explores past trauma or addresses clinical conditions, neurointegration focuses on what your nervous system needs right now: first settling the physiological stress response through targeted breathwork and body practices, and then establishing sustainable daily habits and clear decisions once your energy and cognitive clarity return.

How does the first consultation work, and can I start with just one session?

Yes, you can start with a single consultation. The 60-minute session takes place via video call (Google Meet or Zoom) or in person at my Belgrade office. We explore your current circumstances, assess stress patterns and cognitive load, pinpoint what is draining your energy, and map out immediate practical steps. Continuing is entirely your choice; committing to a multi-week package is never required.

How many sessions are typically needed to see noticeable results?

It depends on your current situation and goals. A single consultation is designed to relieve acute overwhelm quickly, bring mental clarity, and outline concrete next steps. When working through chronic exhaustion or burnout, sustainable habit change takes time: for that, I recommend the four-week support programme (4 sessions with ongoing messaging support) or a flexible 5-session package.

Is there support between sessions?

Yes, continuous support between sessions—answering your questions, tracking micro-habits, and providing personal feedback via Telegram—is an integral part of my four-week programme. For single consultations, I do not provide ongoing messaging between meetings: this gives you the mental breathing room to test and integrate recommendations at your own pace.

Which conditions and issues do you not work with?

My practice is strictly non-medical. I do not work with clinical diagnoses (clinical depression, bipolar disorder, PTSD), chemical dependencies, eating disorders, nor do I work with children or couples. These conditions require clinical medicine and specialized psychiatric care. In acute health emergencies or crisis situations, please contact emergency services immediately by dialling 112.

What formats are consultations available in (online worldwide & in-person in Belgrade)?

I work with adult clients (18+) across two convenient formats. Online sessions are conducted worldwide via Google Meet or Zoom: I regularly support professionals and expats living in Belgrade, London, Berlin, Barcelona, Limassol, and beyond. If you are in Serbia, we can also meet face-to-face: in-person sessions are held at my Belgrade office by advance appointment.

How do I distinguish emotional burnout from everyday tiredness?

Everyday tiredness fades after good sleep and a quiet, restorative weekend. With burnout, ordinary rest no longer works: you wake up already exhausted, feel detached or cynical toward work, carry constant background anxiety, and struggle to concentrate. In my practice, I help you break this cycle and gently restore your nervous system's innate capacity to recover.

How do brain, psyche, and body connect in practice (the triad approach)?

I do not rely on intellectual conversation alone. When your body is locked in chronic tension and breathing is shallow, the brain interprets these physical signals as danger, keeping the nervous system trapped in fight-or-flight mode. In my work, I combine gentle qigong movements and Oxygen Advantage® breathing protocols to calm this physiological stress first, and then apply psychological tools to anchor mental clarity and lasting habits without self-forcing.
